Origin & Purpose

Care, Compassion and Humanity in Action

ELROI CARE TRUST was established in Tiruvallur District, Tamil Nadu, with a sole divine purpose: to serve underprivileged, vulnerable, and disadvantaged people in society. We believe that every human being deserves respect, equal opportunities, and a better quality of life, regardless of their financial situation, physical abilities, age, or background.

Our journey began by observing the severe neglect faced by elderly widows, blind individuals who cannot earn a livelihood, and tribal settlements living in makeshift plastic tents without regular access to clean water or rice. Founder P.K. Immanuel Neas Kumar started personally visiting these doorsteps with sacks of rice and groceries, laying the foundation for a trust that now touches thousands of lives every month.

Our Guiding Conviction

"Together, we can make a difference. Every act of kindness matters. Hunger knows no religion, disability knows no caste. When we extend a bag of rice or hold the hand of a blind elder, we are serving humanity itself."

Our Mission

To serve humanity with kindness, compassion, and dedication by providing essential food, healthcare, clothing, and livelihood support to people in acute need.

Our Vision

To create a compassionate and inclusive society where everyone has the opportunity to live with dignity, equality, and hope.

Dedicated Beneficiaries

Whom ELROI CARE TRUST Supports

Our ongoing outreach programs are tailored specifically to 8 vulnerable groups who need our collective protection and love.

1. Poor & Disadvantaged Families

We provide full monthly ration kits containing rice, dal, and cooking essentials to families living below the poverty line.

2. Persons with Physical Disabilities

Mobility equipment, wheelchair assistance, hygiene supplies, and monthly financial stipends so they never feel abandoned.

3. Blind & Visually Impaired Individuals

Adopting visually impaired persons with regular monthly grocery deliveries directly to their homes and guidance for daily living.

4. Intellectual & Developmental Needs

Assisting special needs individuals and supporting their dedicated family caregivers with food, medical support, and counsel.

5. Destitute Widows & Deserted Women

Supporting widows with nutritious provisions, festive clothing, dresses, and a safe, respectful support network.

6. Elderly Needing Care & Dignity

Home-delivered meals, walking aids, blankets for winters, and regular volunteer check-ins for lonely and abandoned elders.

7. Children from Underprivileged Homes

Educational stationery, evening tuition assistance, celebratory joyful camps, and wholesome food feasts.

8. Tribal & Nomadic Hamlets

Reaching deep into remote settlements with truckloads of grains, fresh produce, clothes, and drinking water assistance.
