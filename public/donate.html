International Donations & FCRA Advisory

In strict compliance with the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act (FCRA) administered by the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India:

  • Non-Resident Indians (NRIs): NRIs holding an Indian Passport donating in Indian Rupees (INR) from their Indian NRE / NRO bank accounts or domestic cards are legally permitted to donate using the Indian bank details above.
