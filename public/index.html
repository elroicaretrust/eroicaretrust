Whom We Support

At ELROI CARE TRUST, no vulnerable person is left behind. We identify and stand with the most disadvantaged members of society across Tamil Nadu.

Poor & Economically Disadvantaged Families

Providing essential grains, pulses, cooking supplies, and emergency sustenance to struggling daily wage households.

Blind & Visually Impaired Individuals

Delivering dedicated monthly provision kits, mobility support, and compassionate direct financial care.

Persons with Physical Disabilities

Facilitating wheelchair access, home healthcare essentials, and dignified livelihood and nutritional backing.

Intellectual & Special Needs Persons

Supporting caregivers with medical necessities, nutrition, and compassionate guidance for special needs individuals.

Destitute Widows & Deserted Women

Ensuring continuous monthly groceries, new dresses and sarees, and emotional security for widowed mothers.

Elderly & Abandoned Senior Citizens

Doorstep distribution of rations, medicines, warm blankets, and respect for lonely and ailing elders.

Underprivileged & Slum Children

Conducting joyful coaching camps, educational kit distribution (school bags, books), and wholesome feasts.

Tribal & Nomadic Settlements

Bringing direct food relief, clothes, shelter materials, and clean drinking support to remote hamlets and tent clusters.
