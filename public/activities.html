FIELD INITIATIVES

Our Charitable Activities

Hands-on social services serving the poorest, the disabled, destitute widows, children, and remote tribal communities.

Lifeline Initiative

Monthly Grocery Provisions & Rice Distribution

For impoverished families, elderly individuals living alone, and daily-wage workers who find no work, procuring basic groceries is a daily battle. Under our Monthly Grocery Provision Program, ELROI CARE TRUST procures and packs comprehensive food ration kits.

What Each Monthly Provision Kit Contains:

  • 10kg to 25kg High Quality Rice
  • 1kg Toor Dal & 1kg Urad Dal
  • Cooking Sunflower Oil (1L)
  • Wheat Flour & Semolina
  • Mustard, Cumin, Chili & Spices
  • Salt, Tea & Sugar
Sponsor a Kit (₹1,500) Feeds an elderly person or family for 1 full month

Real photo: High-grade Toor Dal, Urad Dal, and dry rations packed for distribution.

Special Care & Dignity

Support for Blind & Differently Abled Persons

Individuals suffering from total blindness, severe visual impairment, or physical paralysis are often completely isolated. In rural Tamil Nadu, many cannot reach local government ration shops without assistance.

Founder P.K. Immanuel Neas Kumar and trust volunteers personally bring monthly sacks of rice and groceries directly to their doorstep. We also offer financial sustenance, white canes for mobility, and medical emergency care.

Adopt a Blind / Disabled Individual Inquire (7904947631)

Real photo: Founder P.K. Immanuel Neas Kumar presenting groceries to a wheelchair-bound beneficiary.

Compassion & Honor

Widow Welfare & Abandoned Senior Citizen Care

Widowed mothers and destitute elderly men and women frequently face devastating poverty and rejection. Many live in thatched huts without pensions or family support.

We provide these respected mothers and fathers with unconditional monthly grocery kits, clean new sarees, dresses, dhotis, blankets for cold nights, and regular compassionate visits so they know they are not alone.

Support a Destitute Widow (₹1,500/mo)

Real photo: Doorstep delivery of fresh clothing and grocery bag to an elderly resident.

Future Generation

Children's Development, Education & Joyful Feasts

Children from rural hamlets and impoverished families often lack access to school bags, stationery, and nutritional balance. ELROI CARE TRUST conducts regular children's educational camps, evening study assistance, and celebration gatherings.

During these vibrant camps, children participate in games, moral storytelling, singing, receive school bags and geometry boxes, and sit together for hot, wholesome festive meals prepared with love.

Sponsor a Children's Program

Real photo: Children receiving prizes and gifts, followed by community lunch feast.

Remote Outreach

Tribal & Nomadic Tent Settlement Relief

Nomadic tribes and scheduled communities often live in temporary tarpaulin tents in rural outskirts. They have no steady electricity, regular wages, or kitchen pantries.

ELROI CARE TRUST mobilizes vehicles packed with sacks of rice, fresh tomatoes, vegetables, provisions, blankets, and children's clothing, sitting together with the communities to distribute aid with warmth and respect.
