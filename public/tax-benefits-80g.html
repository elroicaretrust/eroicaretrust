Section 80G Tax Exemption

Support humanity and save tax. All voluntary contributions made to ELROI CARE TRUST are eligible for a 50% deduction under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act.

50% Tax Deduction

Indian citizens, salaried individuals, self-employed professionals, and companies can deduct 50% of the donated amount from their gross taxable income under Chapter VI-A.

Form 10BE Certificate

In accordance with Income Tax guidelines, we file annual donor statements (Form 10BD). Donors receive a valid Form 10BE certificate reflecting automatically in your AIS and Form 26AS.

Instant Stamped Receipt

Immediately upon submitting your contribution and transaction details, generate your official branded 80G donation receipt bearing our registered seal and founder signature.
